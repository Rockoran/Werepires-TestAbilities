# WerePires 1.15 CellPhone final parity checkpoint

Date: 2026-08-15 (America/Chicago)

This checkpoint completes the final parity audit of the native Java port of `CellPhone.sk`.

## Added and corrected

- Recognizes legacy Skript phone sticks by their `phone_*` item model and upgrades them when a color is selected.
- Restores the original purple default and applies color changes to every phone in the player's inventory.
- Adds `/pickup`, `/reject`, and `/cphangup` call aliases.
- Adds favorite-first contact ordering, private nicknames, contact calling, unread direct-message counts, and notification sounds/vibration.
- Adds eight-message direct and group conversation pages with newer/older navigation.
- Adds recent-50 and full click-to-copy transcript exports, capped at 8,000 characters.
- Adds GPS rename, share, delete, limits, and player-relative direction tracking.
- Restores the original limits: 50 GPS pins, 100 notes, 200-character posts, 3-16-character handles, 24-character group names, and 10 group members.
- Adds six-post social pages, all/following feeds, post deletion, and per-character handles, follows, authorship, and reactions.
- Adds a schema migration for handles created by the earlier 1.15 checkpoints.
- Adds group unread counts, message paging, exports, and notification settings.

## Verification

- Clean Maven package completed successfully with Java 21.
- Output remains the development-only `WerePires-1.15-TESTBUILD.jar`.
- The network disable-protocol kill switch remains set to `false` for this test build.
