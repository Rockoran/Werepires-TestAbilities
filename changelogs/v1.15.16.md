# v1.15.16 — MC 1.21.10 — 2026-08-20

## Fixed
- **A ghost can now haunt several people at once.** Haunting was stored as one target per ghost
  (`Map<UUID, UUID>`), so `/ghost haunt` on a second player silently replaced the first. It is now
  a set, and the voice relay sends the same audio to every target.

## Added
- `/ghost haunt all` — haunt everyone currently online.
- `/ghost haunt list` — see who you are haunting.
- `/ghost unhaunt <player>` — release one listener; with no argument it still releases everyone
  and reports how many.

## Changed
- `GhostModeManager`: `getHauntTarget`/`setHauntTarget` replaced by `getHauntTargets`,
  `addHauntTarget`, `removeHauntTarget`, `isHaunting`. `clearHauntTarget` now returns how many
  targets were released.
- `/ghost` tab completion now suggests `all` and `list` after `haunt`, and — after `unhaunt` —
  only the players you are actually haunting, rather than everyone online.

## Notes
- Targets that are offline or not connected to voice chat are skipped individually, so one absent
  listener no longer aborts the relay to the rest.
- When a player disconnects they are removed from every ghost's target set, and ghosts left
  haunting nobody are pruned.
