# v1.15.13 — MC 1.21.10 — 2026-08-20

## Added
- The Fading tome now has a cooldown: `abilities.tome.fading.cooldown` (default `300` seconds).
  A fade still runs to completion once started — the cooldown limits how often you can drop out
  of sight, not how long you can stay there.
- Rockoran bypasses the Fading cooldown entirely and gains **flight + noclip** while faded.
  The `vampiresmp.fading.bypass` permission grants the same to anyone else without a code change.

## Changed
- `GhostModeManager` gained `setExternalNoclip(Player, boolean)` so client-side noclip can be
  toggled without entering full ghost mode. Real ghosts are skipped — their ghost state owns that
  channel, and stomping it would drop them out of noclip mid-haunt.

## Notes
- The bypass perks only ever revoke what they granted: flight is not stripped from a player who
  already had it (creative, or another plugin), and noclip is never sent to an active ghost.
- Perks are cleared on death, quit and session end, so a player cannot be stranded flying.
- This build still carries the `-TESTBUILD` jar suffix, meaning `WerePiresNetwork.PROTOCOL_ENABLED`
  is `false` — no licence check, heartbeat, telemetry or remote disable. That is dev-only state
  inherited from the current branch, not something introduced by this change.
