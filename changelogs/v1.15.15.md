# v1.15.15 — MC 1.21.10 — 2026-08-20

## Added
- **Fae cooldown bargains.** A fae can now trade a permanent cooldown reduction to a specific
  player on a specific ability:

      /pow faedeal cooldown <player> <ability> <percent>
      /pow faedeal cooldown <player> list
      /pow faedeal cooldown <player> clear [ability]

  The fae chooses both the target and the ability, so a bargain can be tailored — a hunter might
  trade for faster Blessing, a vampire for a shorter Lunge.
- Reductions cover **all three cooldown systems**: tome, vampire and werewolf abilities.
  Every one of them funnels through a single `setCooldown`, so there is one place that decides it.
- New `CooldownBoonManager`, persisted to `cooldown_boons.json` — bargains outlive a restart.

## Changed
- `VampireAbilityManager` and `WerewolfAbilityManager` gained `getAllAbilityNames()`, used to
  validate and tab-complete the ability argument against the live registries rather than a
  hardcoded list.

## Notes
- Reductions are capped at **90%** and always leave at least 1 second. A bargain can shorten a
  cooldown, never turn an ability into a free action.
- **Global cooldowns are deliberately untouched.** Storm Call uses a server-wide cooldown shared
  by every vampire; letting one player's personal bargain shorten it would hand the whole team a
  buff that only one of them paid for.
- Granting `0` (or `clear`) revokes a bargain.
