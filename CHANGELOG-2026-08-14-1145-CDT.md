# WerePires test build — 2026-08-14 11:45 CDT

## Bug fixes

- Fixed Vampire Blood being cancelled before its drinking animation could begin.
- Humans can drink Vampire Blood to form thrall bonds; vampires can drink it to restore eight thirst points. Ordinary Crimson Blood remains unavailable to humans.
- Fae-created vampires now keep a `0.001` blood floor and cannot promote or demote outside their bargained stage.
- Added per-bargain life binding: `/pow faedeal vampire <player> <stage> [true|false]`. The optional value defaults to `false`; `true` breaks that bargain if its Fae permanently dies.
- Existing Fae bargain data remains compatible because a missing life-binding value loads as `false`.
- Fire Breath and Magic Fire Breath now share a cooldown family, preventing simultaneous damage streams.
- Renamed the player-facing Blue Fire Breath ability to Magic Fire Breath and replaced soul-fire wording with magic-fire wording.
- Existing `BlueFireBreath` ability tags and configuration are accepted as legacy aliases.
- Added explicit death messages for `/pow permakill` and `/pow admin permakill <player>`.

## Included feature snapshot

This commit also records the previously uncommitted Fae commands, death/heart controls, turn locks, post-turn ability permissions, Scrying, Fading, compatibility networking changes, and their configuration. `graphify-out` is intentionally excluded.
