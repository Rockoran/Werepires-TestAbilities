# WerePires 1.15.6 — 2026-08-15

- Changed Wordle feedback from square symbols to colored letters, including correct duplicate-letter scoring.
- Made right-clicking the air with the cellphone in the main hand reliably open the phone, even when another plugin has already cancelled the interaction.
- Added automatic refreshes for open Connect 4, Tic-tac-toe, and Rock Paper Scissors match screens when either player makes a move, accepts or ends a match.
- Kept the existing per-player `noRefresh` phone preference: players who enable it continue to receive messages without forced screen updates.
- Fixed multiplayer game challenges incorrectly reporting that no contacts were online by reading contacts from the player's UUID record instead of the character-specific game record key.
